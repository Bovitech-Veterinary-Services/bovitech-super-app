Bovitech Practice — Privacy policy

Bovitech Practice is an internal app used only by staff of Bovitech Veterinary Services, 317 Gull Rock Road, Kalgan WA 6330.

What it stores

Client and business contact details (names, phone numbers, email and postal addresses, farm locations, PICs, herd/flock IDs and notes) that Bovitech staff enter for running the practice.

Where it is stored

On the staff device using the app, and in the practice database (Supabase, hosted in Sydney, Australia). Only signed-in, active Bovitech staff can read it.
